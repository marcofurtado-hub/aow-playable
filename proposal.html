Metagame UX
Pillars & Proposal

The pillars: shared code the market already taught our players. The proposal: six concrete moves on our real screens, each with a before/after and its benchmark.

✓W = independently confirmed by Watabe's hands-on UX Assessment (Aug 2026).

Part 1

The Pillars

Six codes to quote in review: convention where the player operates, AoW signature where the player feels.

P1

Battle is the center of gravity

One play button per surface; every screen returns to combat in ≤2 taps. Every element gets a priority level: 1st = the screen's goal, 2nd = relevant, 3rd = hidden behind a tap.

↳ Clash Royale · Brawl Stars: giant play button, under the thumb · ✓W levels of priority per screen
P2

Direction carries meaning

Up = progress (Trophy Road, leaderboard, positive deltas) · right = time (pass, daily, event) · down = loss/exit.

↳ Trophy Road climbs in CR/BS · rank 1 on top · sheets slide from bottom (iOS/Android) · ✓W today TR and BP scroll in opposite directions
P3

Every color has one owner

Yellow play · green gain/claim · gold value (never a button) · purple premium · red alert · blue info. Rarity gray→blue→purple→gold. Value palette: luminance by role, so hierarchy survives in grayscale.

↳ Supercell's color map · mid-core rarity standard · ✓W red=destructive, green=positive, value palette
P4

Short paths, clean home

Fewer taps through contextual shortcuts — never through more fixed buttons. Play in 1 tap, collect in 1, upgrade in 2. And a reach map: a button's importance is inversely proportional to its distance from the thumb — primary actions dock to the bottom, rare/dangerous ones live deliberately far, at the top.

↳ the genre ceiling: Clash Royale · thumb-zone ergonomics (one-handed play)
P5

A badge is a promise

A red dot only when there is a real, executable gain waiting right now. Urgency lives in the countdown, not in the badge.

↳ Supercell discipline — not the 4X noise (a different session format from ours)
P6

One grammar, many systems

Track, card and claim are single components reused by pass, event, daily, Trophy Road and campaign — each dressed as an operation. Same for UI responses: one popup standard, one close pattern (today: 3), one toggle behavior.

↳ Supercell's silent economy for years · ✓W standardize responses & close patterns

03

One ladder per surface

Five progression numbers today — trophies, faction rank, faction level, season XP, stars — with trophies repeated 3× on the main menu ✓W. Proposal: one ladder per surface.

Home
Account trophies — the war ladder (Trophy Road/frontline), the one number every match moves.
Army
Faction mastery — rank + level live in the faction card, never on the home.
Front
Season XP — only on the pass bar. Stars get a sink or leave the top bar.

Reference: CR's trophies + Path of Legends confusion is the cautionary tale; Brawl Stars' trophies vs. mastery split is the solution we copy.

04

A post-match that acts, not just informs

With ~4-minute matches, post-match is the most-seen screen in the metagame. Design it as a 4-beat ritual, crossable in seconds, last beat actionable.

1 · ResultTrophy delta animating up (win) or down (loss) — P2, literally.DEFEAT: SHORT PATH, NO UPSELL
2 · Tracks advanceDaily moves + pass bar fills in front of the player — both on the single track grammar (P6).
3 · LootBlueprints and gold. Here enters the hidden-rarity drop — an armored supply crate cracked open tap by tap.
4 · Next order"Assault reached 8/8" → the upgrade button right there, 1 tap, no trip to the collection. Then back to the War Room.← THE INNOVATION: THE BENCHMARK ONLY INFORMS

Reference: beats = CR/BS; the hidden-rarity drop = Starr Drop (2023), already copied by CR. Beat 4 is where we pass the benchmark.

05

A collection that points to the next step

The build's unit modal (‹ › arrows) is a catalog to leaf through. The player wants one answer: "what can I improve right now?"

Today (build v0.12)

Arrow through units one by one; the payable state looks like everything else.

Proposal

Sort by "actionable first": payable upgrades on top in green (P3), then "almost there", then the rest. Army badge only when something is payable (P5).

Reference: CR's "upgrade available" filter + BS's pending-progress spotlight — the pattern that turns a collection into a queue of decisions.

06

Events and campaign as operations

Siege and the PvE campaign are each being born with their own layout. Run both (plus pass and daily) on one track component, dressed as a military operation.

Event
Horizontal track (P2) + own leaderboard; teaser countdown between events.
Campaign
Lives on the faction card, pays that faction's blueprints — PvE purpose for no-PvP days.
Cost
Built once → every new event is content + skin, not a new system.

Reference: Supercell's one-anatomy passes/events. The operation skin is AoW signature at zero usability cost.
